Why You Only See What You Already Believe

## Description

Seven proofs his new neighbour hated him, and every one was real. Confirmation bias explained in three minutes.

A man keeps a numbered list of everything his neighbour does wrong. You will see how confirmation bias works: a hunch picks the story, then keeps only the facts that agree with it, and the moments that do not fit never make the list. It is one of the best-studied cognitive biases, and it does not mean every suspicion is wrong, only that a suspicion collects its own evidence. If you were searching for cognitive bias explained simply, with a story instead of a lecture, start here.
