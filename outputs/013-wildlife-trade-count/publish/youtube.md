A new study counts at least 107,356 wild species used or traded worldwide: how many species are traded, and what the number does not say.

This explainer shows what was counted (animals, plants and fungi), why the new figure is a broader count and not proof that trade doubled, where the gaps in the data are, and what the authors propose.
