# Asset requests for the owner

The night job appends here every place or object a story needed that it could not build from primitives, furniture
macros (`scripts/furniture.py`) or Kenney models (`assets/kenney-glb`). Make it by hand, then mark the row `done`:
a GLB model dropped into `.claude/skills/code2video-resource-generator/assets/kenney-glb/` becomes `{"t":"prop","n":"<file name>"}`;
a new furniture macro goes into `scripts/furniture.py`.
