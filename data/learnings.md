## 2026-11-14 (night of videos 006, 007)
- **Spec lint and the TTS disagree on speaking speed.** `spec_lint` counts ~3.05 words/s of natural speech (a spec at 2.7 words/s shows "silence ~11%"). The VieNeu English voice with the x1.08 base pace needs about 2.3 words/s; at 2.7 words/s, 13-15 of 58 lines were cut or rushed (006 first render, `voiceover_fit.json`: rushed 13, cut 15) and `verify_bundle video` fails. After trimming to 2.3 words/s: rushed 0, cut 0 (006), rushed 0, cut 0 (007), but the lint now says silence 23-26%. Evidence: outputs/006,007 `voiceover_fit.json`. Proposal for the improvement branch: change the lint's words/s constant to 2.4 for `en`, and write specs to 2.3-2.4 words/s.
- **What gets cut is not only length but stops.** The lines that were cut were short fragments with several full stops ("Can't. The course. Remember?", "A ticket. A course. A year of data.", "Okay. Okay, here. Balance, three thousand."): the TTS pauses at each stop. Joining them with commas into one sentence fixed all of them without changing the word count. Rule for specs: at most one full stop inside a line shorter than 10 words.
- Hook line for 9:16 must be <= 10 words (007 line 1 had 12): split into two cues.
- Ops: `hyperframes render` allows one render per user; a killed render leaves `~/.cache/hyperframes/locks/render.lock*` and the next render waits forever ("Waiting for another render") until the lock is removed. A job started with `nohup ... &` was cancelled with `render_cancelled_parent_exited` when the tool call ended; start it as a background task of the agent instead. `pkill -f` / `ps | grep` patterns that appear in the same command line kill the agent's own shell.
- Playwright's node in the runner image lacks `libatomic.so.1`, so `html-thumbnail/scripts/render_thumbnail.py` fails; `google-chrome --headless --no-sandbox --allow-file-access-from-files --window-size=W,H --screenshot=out.png file://...` works as the fallback (used for 006/007 thumbnails).
